Privacy Policy

Mercury Life AI · Effective November 12, 2026

Support

Data on your device

The app stores tasks, habits, habit logs, goals and Calendar synchronization settings on your device. We do not operate an app backend, require an app account, collect analytics, display advertisements or track you. This version does not send your entries to an AI service.

Optional Calendar access

Mercury Plus subscribers can choose to grant full Calendar access through Apple's permission prompt. The app uses EventKit on your device to create a dedicated Mercury Life AI calendar and synchronize the title and date of your Mercury tasks with their linked events. It does not send calendar contents to us or copy unrelated events into the app.

Your calendar provider, such as iCloud, may synchronize the dedicated calendar according to your device and account settings. Device backups may include app data according to your Apple settings. These services are controlled by you and their providers.

Purchases

Apple processes subscription payments. The app uses StoreKit to check verified subscription transactions and unlock Mercury Plus. We do not receive your payment card details. Apple handles its own account, purchase and transaction data under its privacy policy.

Your choices

You can delete entries in the app, disable Calendar sync, or revoke Calendar permission in iOS Settings. Disabling sync leaves existing linked events in Calendar; you can delete those events or the dedicated calendar yourself. Deleting the app removes its local data, subject to your backups, but does not automatically delete calendar events or cancel subscriptions.

Support and contact

If you email us, we receive the information you choose to send and use it to answer your request. Do not include private calendar contents, passwords or payment details. Contact Iulian Valentin Nedelcu: valentin.nedelcu@icloud.com.

Changes

This page will be updated if the app's data practices change. The effective date appears above.
