XStarCad Privacy Policy

Effective November 14, 2026 · Support

Local drafting data

XStarCad stores drawings, layers, blocks, equipment attributes and document command aliases locally on your device. It maintains local autosave and previous-save recovery copies. No app account or developer backend is required. We do not collect drawing contents, analytics, advertising identifiers or location, and we do not track users. CAD commands and scripts run locally without external code execution or AI services.

Files and printing

You choose whether to import, export or print drawings through Files and AirPrint. Any cloud file provider or printer you select handles files according to its own settings. Device and iCloud backups may include app data under your Apple settings. These choices do not send drawings to the developer.

Previous organizer and purchases

This CAD version replaces Mercury Life AI. It does not request Calendar access, synchronize events, or use the Mercury Plus paywall. Legacy organizer data is not read by this editor. Apple manages any previous subscription and payment information. Manage or cancel subscriptions in iOS Settings. We do not receive payment card details.

Your choices

Export separate JSON projects, use New to start another autosaved drawing, and Recover to restore the previous save. Delete exported files using Files. Deleting the application removes its local container subject to device backups; it does not delete exported cloud files or cancel subscriptions.
