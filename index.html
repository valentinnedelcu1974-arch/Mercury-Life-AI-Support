Getting started

Use Tasks to add, edit and complete tasks with a due date and time. Track daily habits, update goal progress and run a 25-minute focus session. These core features work without an account.

Mercury Plus and Calendar

Mercury Plus is an optional auto-renewable annual subscription. The price is displayed by the App Store before purchase. Open Today, tap the Calendar & Plus icon, subscribe and then tap Enable Calendar Sync. Calendar access is requested only after you choose to enable it.

Sync keeps Mercury task titles and due times aligned with linked 30-minute events in a dedicated Mercury Life AI calendar while the app is open. Only Mercury-linked events are changed. Other calendar events are not imported or modified. If both sides changed, the most recent edit wins.

Completing or deleting a task removes its linked event when Calendar access is available. Deleting an event in Calendar keeps the task and turns off sync for that task. Disabling sync or ending your subscription leaves existing events in Calendar and pauses synchronization. Review sensitive or important appointments in Apple Calendar.

Restore or cancel

Use Restore Purchases on the Mercury Plus screen with the Apple Account that purchased the subscription. Manage or cancel in iOS Settings > Apple Account > Subscriptions. Subscriptions renew automatically unless canceled before renewal. Deleting the app does not cancel a subscription.
