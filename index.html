XStarCad

Native 2D technical drafting for iPhone and iPad. Replaces Mercury Life AI.

Privacy Policy · Terms of Use

Get started

Use the drawing toolbar and X/Y fields. Horizontal toolbars scroll on smaller screens. Open the CAD main menu for File, Edit, View, Draw, Modify, Layers, Annotate, Blocks, Measure and Tools.

Enter LINE 0,0 100,50, PLINE 0,0 100,0 100,100 CLOSE or INSERT "PV panel" 100,100 40 PV01. HELP lists supported commands. Select objects on the canvas, in the Objects panel or with SELECT. Layer styles, locks, blocks and equipment attributes are stored in the native project.

Electrical and engineering drafting

Libraries include electrical, mechanical and civil symbols, HV/MV/LV equipment, PV, BESS, grounding and SCADA. AUDIT checks duplicate tags, missing parameters and unconnected electrical ports. Ports connect at matching wire endpoints; crossings are not junctions. These are illustrative drafting checks, not engineering compliance calculations or certified designs.

Files and layout

Changes autosave locally. Export JSON for separate projects; Recover restores the previous valid autosave. Import supported 2D DXF/JSON. Export JSON, DXF, vector PDF, SVG and equipment/quantity CSV. Model/Layout previews an A4 PDF sheet. SHEET sets title and scale; PLOT opens AirPrint. Check fixed-scale sheets for clipping.

Arcs and splines use segmented geometry. DWG, STEP/IFC, 3D solids, extrusion, boolean modeling and XLSX are not included. JSON preserves app attributes and blocks; DXF exchanges supported geometry.

Previous Mercury Life AI users

The CAD editor requires no subscription and does not synchronize Calendar. Legacy organizer data is not used. Manage or cancel any earlier Mercury Plus subscription in iOS Settings > Apple Account > Subscriptions. Deleting the app does not cancel subscriptions.
